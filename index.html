Free Copy-Editing Block Starter Kit

Small boosts for stuck authors and journals: 40 copy-editing prompts, blank-outline heavy templates, revision checklists, and a per-project mood switch. Loads from JSON, works offline, no accounts, ever.

Choose a mood for this project

Picked mood and checklist ticks are saved in your browser only (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded; there are no accounts.

40 Copy-Editing Blocking Prompts

Pick one prompt, do it, stop if you want. These are for the stuck moments, not a to-do list.

Blank-Outline Templates

Outlines with heavy blanks: fill the blanks and the structure does the rest. Copy buttons give you a plain-text version.

Revision Checklists

One pass, one job. Tick as you go; ticks persist locally.

Printer-Friendly Section

Use your browser's Print button (or Ctrl/Cmd+P). The [Print View] below prints the whole kit — all 40 prompts, every blank outline, and both checklists — in compact text.
